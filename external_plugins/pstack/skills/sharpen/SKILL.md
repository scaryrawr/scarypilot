description: "Sharpen a task before starting: the expert approach, what the user is really after, and the right level of specification. Use for /sharpen."
---

Before starting, ask yourself how a top expert would approach this task, using raw sources, real methods, and an outside check that the result is right. Identify what the user is really after and whether the task is over- or underspecified. Loosen constraints that do not serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do. Leave choices that are the user's to make open instead of deciding them.

Fold in only what makes the work better, not bigger. Keep one outside check that the result is right, without narrating the framing, then do the work. Do not hand back a rewritten prompt. Respect the requested mode and scope. A planning request remains planning, and sharpening does not authorize implementation, publication, or irreversible actions.
